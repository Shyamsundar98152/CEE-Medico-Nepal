What is the CEE Nepal Medical Entrance Exam?

The Common Entrance Examination (CEE) is the medical entrance exam conducted by the Medical Education Commission (MEC) of Nepal for admission into MBBS, BDS and allied health programs in Nepali medical colleges. Every year, thousands of MBBS entrance Nepal aspirants sit for the CEE, making structured CEE preparation Nepal — not just memorization — the deciding factor between a seat and a retake.

Common Questions About CEE Nepal Preparation

What is CEE in Nepal?

CEE (Common Entrance Examination) is the medical entrance exam conducted by the Medical Education Commission (MEC) of Nepal for admission into MBBS, BDS and other medical programs across Nepali medical colleges.

How can I prepare for MEC CEE for free?

Use CEE Medico Nepal's daily and weekly mock tests, subject-wise MCQs with explanations, downloadable notes for Biology, Chemistry and Physics, and previous year CEE question papers — all free to access.

Is CEE Medico Nepal useful for NEET and USMLE as well?

Yes. Alongside CEE/MEC preparation, the platform hosts NEET-pattern mock tests and notes for cross-border applicants, plus USMLE and NMCLE MCQs for licensing exam preparation after MBBS.

What subjects does the CEE Nepal exam cover?

Physics, Chemistry, Botany and Zoology (Biology), and general IQ-based questions, tested through objective multiple-choice questions within a fixed time limit.
