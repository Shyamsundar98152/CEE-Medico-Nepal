About CEE Medico Nepal

We are dedicated to providing high-quality, accessible educational resources for medical aspirants in Nepal. Every subject module is organized by academic track so you can review foundational sciences, diagnostic pathology/microbiology, and core clinical decision-making in order.

Recommended Practice Flow

  • Build core concepts in Anatomy, Physiology & Biochemistry.
  • Reinforce disease mechanisms via Pathology, Microbiology & Pharmacology.
  • Apply clinical reasoning across Medicine, Surgery, Obs/Gynae & specialties.
  • Tap "Mark Done" on any card to track your personal subject checklist.
